# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis | Excel

One of my first data-analysis projects, completed entirely in Microsoft Excel.

## Project overview
I explored Superstore order lines to review sales and profit across categories, regions, states, and customers.

## Tools and methods
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and distinct-count KPI
- Dashboard charts and timeline filters

## Key results
- 9,994 order lines and 5,009 distinct orders
- Total sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total profit: 286,397.02
- Overall profit margin: 12.47%
- Technology had the highest sales and profit by category
- Tables recorded a loss of 17,725.48
